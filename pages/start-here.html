Start Here: learn MYTH 2.0 solo

Your goal for the first session is to learn the MYTH 2.0 sequence of play, use your matching 2.0 hero and Darkness cards correctly, and build confidence through one complete introductory scenario.

Ruleset lock: MYTH 2.0. This guide uses the MYTH 2.0 rulebook and matching MYTH 2.0 cards as its authority. Do not apply original first-edition rules or Dawn of Heroes / Orphans of Ovenstein procedures. If a detail has not yet been checked against the 2.0 materials, it is explicitly marked unverified rather than guessed.

Before you set up

  • Use the MYTH 2.0 rulebook and matching 2.0 cards for the selected hero, enemies and scenario.
  • Choose a party of multiple heroes for multi-handed solo play, using the party-size and hero-selection requirements stated in the MYTH 2.0 scenario and rules. Do not assume a first-edition party limit.
  • Gather the matching MYTH 2.0 deck and required components for every selected hero, plus the scenario, Darkness and enemy components.
  • Separate hero decks, Darkness cards, monster cards, trap cards, quest/scenario cards, items, tokens, tiles and dice. Keep each deck distinct.
  • Keep the rulebook and a notepad close; write down questions rather than interrupting every turn to solve them.
  • Use the exact setup and victory conditions printed in your scenario.

Set up for multi-handed solo play

  1. Check the scenario first. Confirm its permitted party size, hero-selection instructions, starting positions, required decks and special setup rules in MYTH 2.0.
  2. Give each hero a separate play area. Keep each hero’s cards, resources, health and conditions associated with that character; do not merge hero decks or trackers.
  3. Make a party tracker. Write each hero’s name, position, health, resources, ongoing effects and objective role on a reference sheet.
  4. Plan as a team. Decide which hero will handle each need, but resolve every card, cost, timing window and effect according to its MYTH 2.0 text.
  5. Run the opposition impartially. Resolve Darkness and enemies from the actual 2.0 rules and cards, even when a different result would help your party.
Party-size accuracy: The guide will not prescribe an exact number of heroes until the MYTH 2.0 rules/scenario being taught has been checked. Use the number permitted by that specific scenario.

Next setup step: Before placing enemies, follow the Darkness and enemy setup checklist to prepare the Darkness board, matching monster cards and miniatures, traps, lairs and scenario-required tokens.

The learning sequence

1

Understand the objective

Read the scenario's setup, objectives, failure conditions and special rules first. These determine what a useful turn looks like.

2

Learn the hero's action economy

Inspect your hero deck and identify what cards do, what they cost, when they can be played and how they refresh. Use the timing, costs and terminology printed in MYTH 2.0 only.

3

Resolve one complete turn

Follow the printed phase order. Finish every effect before moving to the next step; this makes timing errors easier to spot.

4

Run the enemy system exactly

Resolve the Darkness or enemy procedure using its actual cards and rules. Do not choose enemy moves by intuition unless the rules explicitly ask you to decide.

5

Review one decision

After the turn, ask: what was my goal, what did my action cost, what did the enemy system do, and what would I change next time?

What to learn first

  1. Multi-hero setup and scenario objectives.
  2. Movement and board positioning.
  3. Each hero’s cards and costs, plus how the heroes support one another.
  4. Attack, defence, damage and enemy removal.
  5. Darkness activation and how the game escalates.
  6. End-of-round checks, victory and defeat.

Solo play: controlling the game, not cheating it

In multi-handed solo play, you make the decisions for every hero in the party. The challenge is to apply enemy procedures consistently and avoid giving the heroes information or leniency that the rules do not grant. If a rule is ambiguous, record the question and use one consistent provisional interpretation until you can verify it.

Important: This is a learning guide, not a replacement for your scenario rules. Exact timing, action costs, enemy priorities and victory conditions will be added only after checking the MYTH 2.0 rulebook, 2.0 cards and relevant FAQ.
