HERO TOOLKIT · 04

Heroes, cards and equipment

This section will grow into a practical playbook for the heroes and components in your collection. The first rule is to identify the edition and exact card set before comparing abilities.

What every hero guide will include

  • Role and strengths: what the hero is designed to do well, and where they are vulnerable.
  • Deck anatomy: card categories, costs, timing windows and resource requirements.
  • Turn planning: ways to sequence cards while keeping enough flexibility for threats.
  • Combos: combinations supported by the actual card text and rules.
  • Solo positioning: how to protect objectives and manage enemy attention when no other player can cover you.
  • Growth and equipment: how items, upgrades and campaign rewards change the hero's decisions.

Research queue

  1. Inventory the original core heroes and their matching decks.
  2. Transcribe and explain the rules-relevant wording of key cards without reproducing whole copyrighted card sets.
  3. Identify high-value card combinations and common sequencing mistakes.
  4. Compare each hero's strengths in solo play and identify any official solo-specific guidance.
  5. Add expansion heroes and equipment once the core guide is useful.
Collection-first approach: Since you own nearly all the game, the aim is to help you use your physical components. No proxy printing is assumed. Any missing components or edition mismatches will be listed explicitly.

Edition labels

The original MERCS-era MYTH, MYTH 2.0 and Dawn of Heroes must not be treated as interchangeable. The official Ulisses MYTH page describes a cooperative game in which heroes use distinct decks and spend Action Points through cards; the Journeyman FAQ also lists 2.0 rulebooks and separate hero-deck materials.

Sources: Ulisses Spiele — MYTH and MYTH: Journeyman FAQ.
