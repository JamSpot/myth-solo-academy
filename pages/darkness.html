TACTICAL SYSTEMS · 03

Understanding the MYTH 2.0 Darkness

The enemy system is the heart of solo play. The aim is to learn its triggers and priorities so you can predict risk without inventing enemy behaviour.

Ruleset lock: MYTH 2.0. This guide uses the MYTH 2.0 rulebook and matching MYTH 2.0 cards as its authority. Do not apply original first-edition rules or Dawn of Heroes / Orphans of Ovenstein procedures. If a detail has not yet been checked against the 2.0 materials, it is explicitly marked unverified rather than guessed.

Set up the Darkness area and enemy components

Use this as a table-side checklist alongside the MYTH 2.0 rulebook. The scenario can add, remove or change components, so its setup instructions take precedence where they say so.

  1. Read the scenario before sorting cards. Identify the exact scenario/quest, the required realm tiles, objective markers, starting positions, enemy types, lairs, traps and any special setup instructions.
  2. Put the Darkness board beside the play area. Keep its card spaces, counters and reference areas visible and reachable. Use the printed board labels rather than creating your own spaces.
  3. Prepare the correct Darkness deck. Use the deck specified by the MYTH 2.0 scenario or rules. Shuffle it when instructed, place it in the correct draw area and keep a discard pile separate. Do not combine boss or expansion decks unless the rules tell you to.
  4. Prepare monster cards and miniatures as a matched set. For each enemy type named by the scenario, keep its MYTH 2.0 monster card with the correct miniatures and any required lair or spawn markers. Check that the card matches the miniature/type before play.
  5. Prepare traps and trap markers. Keep the trap deck and its markers together. Only place or reveal traps as directed by the scenario, tile icons and rules.
  6. Prepare treasure and other tokens. Put the required treasure counters, status tokens and other shared supplies in distinct piles so they can be found quickly. Do not put a token on a tile merely because you have one; check the tile legend and scenario.
  7. Build the realm board from the scenario. Lay out the specified tile sides and sizes, connect edges as instructed, and then inspect every tile icon. Resolve mandatory icons and any optional choices according to MYTH 2.0 and the scenario before deciding that the tile is ready.
  8. Populate enemies from the actual setup instructions. Match each required lair, hunting pack or other enemy placement to its tile icon and scenario instruction. Use the correct monster card for the type placed; don't choose a weaker or more convenient enemy just to make the setup easier.
  9. Do a final audit. Compare the table with the scenario: heroes and starting positions, tile layout, objectives, enemy groups, lairs, traps, Darkness deck, cards, tokens and any scenario timer or special rule.
Verified setup reference: The MYTH 2.0 rules sample identifies a Darkness board, a Darkness deck, monster cards and miniatures, lair tokens, treasure counters, a trap deck and trap tokens as Darkness setup components. The exact deck, starting marker and placement details must be checked in the full rules and the scenario you are playing.

Learn what each card is for

Before you shuffle or place a card, identify its job. Keep these groups separate on the table:

  • Darkness cards: the instructions for Darkness events and enemy resolution. Read the whole card in its printed order whenever it is drawn.
  • Monster cards: the reference for the matching enemy type. Keep the card accessible while its enemies are in play; consult its attributes and special instructions rather than relying on memory.
  • Trap cards: used by the trap procedure. Keep the trap deck separate from the Darkness deck and follow the relevant trigger and resolution rules.
  • Hero decks: each selected hero uses the correct MYTH 2.0 deck. Never merge several heroes' decks into one pile.
  • Item cards: use only when gained, equipped, bought or otherwise introduced by the scenario and rules. Keep unowned items out of a hero's active inventory.
  • Quest/scenario cards: define the current objective, setup and special conditions. These are the first cards to read, not a surprise to discover halfway through play.
  • Additional/expansion cards: include only if the selected scenario or rules call for them. Do not automatically mix every card you own into the base setup.

Card-reading routine

  1. Read the card title and identify its type.
  2. Read all timing words, conditions and costs before resolving it.
  3. Identify who or what the instruction affects.
  4. Check whether it changes movement, targeting, spawning, status, resources or a later phase.
  5. Resolve the full instruction in order, then move the card or marker to its correct area.
Important boundary: This is a setup framework, not yet a complete card-by-card catalogue. Exact symbol meanings, monster priorities, Darkness card timing and tile-icon placement will be documented from the MYTH 2.0 rulebook and matching cards before being labelled confirmed.

How to study enemy behaviour

1

Identify the trigger

What event causes the enemy procedure to activate? Check the scenario, phase sequence, cards and any special enemy rules.

2

Read the actual priority

Determine which enemy, target or action is selected and in what order. If priorities are listed, follow their order exactly.

3

Resolve legal behaviour

Apply movement, target eligibility, range, line of sight and special abilities. If a preferred action is illegal, use the rulebook's fallback rather than guessing.

4

Resolve all consequences

Apply attacks, damage, conditions, spawns and other triggered effects in the correct order, including any new triggers those effects create.

Build a threat map before acting

At the start of a decision, scan the board in this order:

  1. Immediate danger: enemies already able to attack or threaten your hero.
  2. Trigger risk: actions that activate the Darkness or advance a pressure track.
  3. Position: routes, chokepoints, terrain and objective access.
  4. Escalation: lairs, captains or other mechanisms that can make future turns harder.
  5. Recovery: legal opportunities to reposition, defend, refresh or restore resources.
Solo discipline: Resolve enemy actions as a procedure, not as a narrative choice. Predict the result before you act, then compare your prediction with the actual rules resolution. This is how the guide will develop reliable tactical examples.

MYTH 2.0 table-side enemy procedure

Use the sequence printed in the MYTH 2.0 rulebook, the active Darkness card, the relevant monster card and scenario instructions. This page deliberately does not import the original-edition community procedure: each priority and timing rule must be checked against the 2.0 text.

  1. Identify the exact trigger. Check what caused a Darkness response and whether the rulebook, scenario or card changes the normal sequence.
  2. Read the active 2.0 instructions. Use the current Darkness card and any specified monster or spawn instructions; don't substitute a generic remembered order.
  3. Resolve enemy selection and priority exactly. Read the relevant MYTH 2.0 monster card. Apply its target and movement priorities in printed order; do not choose a more convenient target for the hero.
  4. Check legal movement. Apply the 2.0 movement rules, board restrictions, range and line-of-sight rules as written. If the first choice cannot be resolved, follow the stated fallback in the 2.0 rules rather than inventing one.
  5. Resolve attacks and card effects. Use MYTH 2.0 attack, defence, damage, condition and timing rules, including any monster-specific exceptions.
  6. Resolve spawns and follow-on effects. Follow the exact spawn instruction and legal placement rules. Recheck for additional triggers in the order specified by 2.0.
  7. Check the end condition. Return to the scenario's objectives and failure conditions after all required effects are complete.
Fair-play test: Before resolving an enemy, state the rule/card that governs its target, movement, attack and any spawn. If you cannot point to the MYTH 2.0 source, mark the question unverified and use a consistent temporary ruling until it can be checked.

Enemy audit sheet

  • Trigger and activation order: rulebook / scenario / Darkness card.
  • Enemy's target and movement priorities: matching MYTH 2.0 monster card.
  • Legal route, range and line of sight: MYTH 2.0 movement/combat rules.
  • Attack, damage and conditions: MYTH 2.0 combat rules and card text.
  • Spawns and chained effects: exact 2.0 spawn and timing instructions.

Research standard: Community wikis and first-edition discussions may help locate a question, but they are not authority for this MYTH 2.0 guide. Any confirmed rule will be tied to the relevant 2.0 page or card; unresolved details stay labelled as such.
