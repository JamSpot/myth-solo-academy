TACTICAL SYSTEMS · 03

Understanding the MYTH 2.0 Darkness

The enemy system is the heart of solo play. The aim is to learn its triggers and priorities so you can predict risk without inventing enemy behaviour.

Ruleset lock: MYTH 2.0. This guide uses the MYTH 2.0 rulebook and matching MYTH 2.0 cards as its authority. Do not apply original first-edition rules or Dawn of Heroes / Orphans of Ovenstein procedures. If a detail has not yet been checked against the 2.0 materials, it is explicitly marked unverified rather than guessed.

How to study enemy behaviour

1

Identify the trigger

What event causes the enemy procedure to activate? Check the scenario, phase sequence, cards and any special enemy rules.

2

Read the actual priority

Determine which enemy, target or action is selected and in what order. If priorities are listed, follow their order exactly.

3

Resolve legal behaviour

Apply movement, target eligibility, range, line of sight and special abilities. If a preferred action is illegal, use the rulebook's fallback rather than guessing.

4

Resolve all consequences

Apply attacks, damage, conditions, spawns and other triggered effects in the correct order, including any new triggers those effects create.

Build a threat map before acting

At the start of a decision, scan the board in this order:

  1. Immediate danger: enemies already able to attack or threaten your hero.
  2. Trigger risk: actions that activate the Darkness or advance a pressure track.
  3. Position: routes, chokepoints, terrain and objective access.
  4. Escalation: lairs, captains or other mechanisms that can make future turns harder.
  5. Recovery: legal opportunities to reposition, defend, refresh or restore resources.
Solo discipline: Resolve enemy actions as a procedure, not as a narrative choice. Predict the result before you act, then compare your prediction with the actual rules resolution. This is how the guide will develop reliable tactical examples.

MYTH 2.0 table-side enemy procedure

Use the sequence printed in the MYTH 2.0 rulebook, the active Darkness card, the relevant monster card and scenario instructions. This page deliberately does not import the original-edition community procedure: each priority and timing rule must be checked against the 2.0 text.

  1. Identify the exact trigger. Check what caused a Darkness response and whether the rulebook, scenario or card changes the normal sequence.
  2. Read the active 2.0 instructions. Use the current Darkness card and any specified monster or spawn instructions; don't substitute a generic remembered order.
  3. Resolve enemy selection and priority exactly. Read the relevant MYTH 2.0 monster card. Apply its target and movement priorities in printed order; do not choose a more convenient target for the hero.
  4. Check legal movement. Apply the 2.0 movement rules, board restrictions, range and line-of-sight rules as written. If the first choice cannot be resolved, follow the stated fallback in the 2.0 rules rather than inventing one.
  5. Resolve attacks and card effects. Use MYTH 2.0 attack, defence, damage, condition and timing rules, including any monster-specific exceptions.
  6. Resolve spawns and follow-on effects. Follow the exact spawn instruction and legal placement rules. Recheck for additional triggers in the order specified by 2.0.
  7. Check the end condition. Return to the scenario's objectives and failure conditions after all required effects are complete.
Fair-play test: Before resolving an enemy, state the rule/card that governs its target, movement, attack and any spawn. If you cannot point to the MYTH 2.0 source, mark the question unverified and use a consistent temporary ruling until it can be checked.

Enemy audit sheet

  • Trigger and activation order: rulebook / scenario / Darkness card.
  • Enemy's target and movement priorities: matching MYTH 2.0 monster card.
  • Legal route, range and line of sight: MYTH 2.0 movement/combat rules.
  • Attack, damage and conditions: MYTH 2.0 combat rules and card text.
  • Spawns and chained effects: exact 2.0 spawn and timing instructions.

Research standard: Community wikis and first-edition discussions may help locate a question, but they are not authority for this MYTH 2.0 guide. Any confirmed rule will be tied to the relevant 2.0 page or card; unresolved details stay labelled as such.

Research targets for the advanced guide
