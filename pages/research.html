Research backlog

  1. Build a page-referenced MYTH 2.0 rules index, recording rulebook version and any official errata or FAQ clarifications.
  2. Locate and cross-check the official MYTH 2.0 rules, matching card decks, FAQs and scenario documents.
  3. Build a component inventory covering the original core game and expansions.
  4. Document setup, turn order, action economy and victory/failure conditions with page references.
  5. Map Darkness triggers and enemy priorities by enemy type, including edge cases.
  6. Write a complete first scenario walkthrough and verify every value against source material.
  7. Research expansion rules, heroes, lairs, bosses, items and campaign interactions.
  8. Maintain a change log whenever a rule interpretation is corrected.

How claims will be labelled

  • Confirmed: supported by the MYTH 2.0 rulebook, a matching 2.0 card, or official clarification.
  • Cross-check: plausible, but awaiting a second source or page-level verification.
  • Community interpretation: a reported interpretation, not an official ruling.
  • Solo aid: an optional house rule or teaching tool, clearly separated from official rules.
Copyright and source handling: This is an independent fan guide. It will link to legitimate sources, explain rules in original wording and avoid reproducing entire rulebooks or card sets.
