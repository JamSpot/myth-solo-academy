Research library

A transparent source list for the guide. Official rules and product material take priority; community discussions can help locate questions but are not automatically treated as rules authority.

Core sources

  • Ulisses Spiele — MYTH official page
    Current publisher overview, high-level game description, hero deck concept and links to official materials. Useful for identifying the Ulisses-era MYTH line.
  • MYTH: Journeyman FAQ
    Official FAQ about Journeyman fulfilment and 2.0 materials. It identifies separate rules, hero decks, item decks and additional cards.
  • Ulisses Games — Myth (US)
    Overview of the Dawn of Heroes version and its hero line. Treat as a distinct product/version rather than assuming it matches the original MERCS box.
  • Original MYTH Kickstarter
    Historical source for the MERCS Miniatures original project and its components.
  • Designing a New Dawn for Myth
    Publisher explanation of Dawn of Heroes and its relationship to earlier MYTH material.

Research backlog

  1. Confirm the exact original core rulebook version and any errata.
  2. Locate official original rules, FAQs and scenario documents that are publicly accessible.
  3. Build a component inventory covering the original core game and expansions.
  4. Document setup, turn order, action economy and victory/failure conditions with page references.
  5. Map Darkness triggers and enemy priorities by enemy type, including edge cases.
  6. Write a complete first scenario walkthrough and verify every value against source material.
  7. Research expansion rules, heroes, lairs, bosses, items and campaign interactions.
  8. Maintain a change log whenever a rule interpretation is corrected.

How claims will be labelled

  • Confirmed: supported by a rulebook, official FAQ or official publisher document for the named edition.
  • Cross-check: plausible, but awaiting a second source or page-level verification.
  • Community interpretation: a reported interpretation, not an official ruling.
  • Solo aid: an optional house rule or teaching tool, clearly separated from official rules.
Copyright and source handling: This is an independent fan guide. It will link to legitimate sources, explain rules in original wording and avoid reproducing entire rulebooks or card sets.
