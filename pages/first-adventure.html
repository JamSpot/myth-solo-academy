BEGINNER PATH · 02

Your first MYTH 2.0 solo adventure

Use this teaching script with the MYTH 2.0 rulebook, 2.0 cards and a scenario compatible with that ruleset. Exact procedures must be checked against those materials.

Ruleset lock: MYTH 2.0. This guide uses the MYTH 2.0 rulebook and matching MYTH 2.0 cards as its authority. Do not apply original first-edition rules or Dawn of Heroes / Orphans of Ovenstein procedures. If a detail has not yet been checked against the 2.0 materials, it is explicitly marked unverified rather than guessed.
Choose a short introductory scenario. Prefer one with a clear objective and limited special rules. Set out only the components it asks for, then read the entire scenario before beginning.

Manage several heroes at once

In multi-handed play, treat each hero as a separate character while planning the party as one team. Keep individual cards, resources, health, conditions and position distinct. Before acting, check whether a hero can support another character without violating MYTH 2.0 timing, range, target or card requirements.

  • Track each hero’s current state separately.
  • Plan a party-wide objective, then choose the next action for one hero.
  • Check the acting hero’s exact card text, cost, target, timing and legal range.
  • After the action, update only the state that the rules say changes.
  • Before the Darkness response, scan every hero and the board so no legal trigger or target is overlooked.
  • Resolve all mandatory enemy behaviour without adjusting it to compensate for controlling multiple heroes.

Walk through a turn

1

Read the current state

Check every hero’s position, health, available resources and ongoing effects; identify nearby enemies, obstacles, objectives and any effects that persist from the previous turn.

2

State your intention

Choose a party-wide aim, then decide which hero is best placed to act next: approach an objective, create space, remove a threat, protect a vulnerable position or prepare for a likely enemy response.

3

Check card timing and costs

Before playing a card, verify its requirements, target, range, cost and timing. Resolve the full card text and all resulting effects in the prescribed order.

4

Resolve movement and combat

Use the MYTH 2.0 movement and combat rules. Measure or count spaces consistently, confirm line of sight and range where relevant, and apply modifiers before determining the result.

5

Resolve the Darkness response

Follow the correct enemy cards, triggers and priority rules. Apply spawning, movement, attacks and status effects only as directed by the rules; do not skip a trigger because it seems inconvenient.

6

Check objectives and end conditions

Resolve end-of-turn or end-of-round effects, then check whether the scenario's success or failure conditions have been met.

A simple decision checklist

  • Does this action advance the scenario objective?
  • What resource or future option am I spending?
  • Will this action trigger an enemy response or other consequence?
  • Does this leave another hero exposed or use a resource they need?
  • Have I resolved all mandatory effects before moving on?

After your first session

Record the rule questions that slowed you down, which cards were difficult to evaluate, how well the heroes supported one another, and when the enemy response surprised you. Those notes will become the priority list for the next iteration of the guide.
