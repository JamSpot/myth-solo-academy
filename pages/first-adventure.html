BEGINNER PATH · 02

Your first solo adventure

Use this as a teaching script alongside the scenario and rulebook. It is intentionally focused on the decision process; edition-specific procedures must be confirmed from your own components.

Choose a short introductory scenario. Prefer one with a clear objective and limited special rules. Set out only the components it asks for, then read the entire scenario before beginning.

Walk through a turn

1

Read the current state

Check your hero's position, health and available resources; identify nearby enemies, obstacles, objectives and any effects that persist from the previous turn.

2

State your intention

Choose one immediate aim: approach an objective, create space, remove a threat, protect a vulnerable position or prepare for a likely enemy response.

3

Check card timing and costs

Before playing a card, verify its requirements, target, range, cost and timing. Resolve the full card text and all resulting effects in the prescribed order.

4

Resolve movement and combat

Use the movement and combat rules for your edition. Measure or count spaces consistently, confirm line of sight and range where relevant, and apply modifiers before determining the result.

5

Resolve the Darkness response

Follow the correct enemy cards, triggers and priority rules. Apply spawning, movement, attacks and status effects only as directed by the rules; do not skip a trigger because it seems inconvenient.

6

Check objectives and end conditions

Resolve end-of-turn or end-of-round effects, then check whether the scenario's success or failure conditions have been met.

A simple decision checklist

  • Does this action advance the scenario objective?
  • What resource or future option am I spending?
  • Will this action trigger an enemy response or other consequence?
  • Can I finish the intended sequence without leaving my hero exposed?
  • Have I resolved all mandatory effects before moving on?

After your first session

Record the rule questions that slowed you down, which cards were difficult to evaluate, and when the enemy response surprised you. Those notes will become the priority list for the next iteration of the guide.

Research in progress: Exact worked examples, costs, ranges and enemy-priority diagrams will be added after the original rulebook and scenario text have been verified. No made-up values are used in this first version.
